// WHAT YOU'LL DO

Climb like your grip depends on it

Inspired by the tension of the original, rebuilt as a quick-to-launch browser tribute with its own routes, creatures and tricks.

01

Two-hand grip

Left and right click grab independently. Every hold drains stamina — chalk up, rest on a ledge, or fall.

02

Routes that never repeat

Vertical, diagonal, zig-zag and long sideways traverses wind up a 700 m silo. Read the wall, pick your line.

03

Parkour districts

Every 150 m the holds vanish: sprint and leap across rooftops, towers, scaffolds and cranes.

04

Something is climbing

Past 150 m a hunter follows you up the wall. Find the dart gun, slow it, keep moving.

05

Momentum & inertia

Pull, lunge, swing and throw yourself between holds. Elevator ledges and pipe catwalks keep you honest.

06

Pixel-dither horror

Low-res rendering, fog, flashlight cone and a handmade sound mix tuned for dread, not noise.

// 150 M AND ABOVE

It heard you climbing

A many-legged hunter drops into the shaft below and crawls toward you, faster the higher you go. Every hit knocks you off the wall.

YOUR ANSWER:

Grab the dart pistol from the floor at the start. Equip it, and your right hand is busy — climb one-handed or keep shooting. A hit slows the creature for 6 seconds.

// QUESTIONS

FAQ

Is this the official White Knuckle?

No. This is an unofficial fan game made as a tribute. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the developers or publishers of White Knuckle, and it uses no assets from the original. If you enjoy it, go play the original.

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs in your browser — no installer, no account, nothing to buy.

What do I need to play?

A desktop or laptop with a mouse and keyboard and a modern browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Touch devices aren't supported.

Is my progress saved?

Your best height is saved in your browser. Each run starts from the bottom.

Does it have sound?

Yes — grabs, jumps, footsteps, the dart gun and the things in the dark. Play with headphones.
